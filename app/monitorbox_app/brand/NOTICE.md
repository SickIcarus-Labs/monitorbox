The lighthouse mark (mark.svg) and its PNG renderings (icon-192, icon-512, icon-maskable-512, apple-touch-icon) are MonitorBox's
own artwork, lifted unchanged from the v3 UI source (monitorbox-modules, sources/ui/1.1.14-build27). favicon-64.png is icon-192 resized.

sora.ttf (Sora) and figtree.ttf (Figtree) are variable fonts from the Google Fonts repository (google/fonts, ofl/sora and ofl/figtree), unchanged,
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL-sora.txt, OFL-figtree.txt). They are served from the box itself so the page never calls out to a font host.
