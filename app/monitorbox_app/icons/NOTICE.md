Service icons are bundled for reliable outage-time rendering. Simple Icons assets
come from https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons (CC0-1.0); additional app
icons come from https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons under that
project's published license. Retrieved 2026-08-16; about 90 more homelab app icons added 2026-10-15 from the same set.

Single-color Simple Icons marks use the corresponding primary brand color from
Simple Icons metadata; multi-color Dashboard Icons assets remain as provided.
MonitorBox never fetches these icons from a monitored service at runtime.
